Can Records Be Manipulated?

No. Every transaction (like creating or transferring a batch) is cryptographically signed and linked to the previous one, forming a chain. Any attempt to alter past data would break this chain, and the change would be instantly rejected by the entire network. The history you see is permanent and verifiable by anyone, ensuring complete integrity.
